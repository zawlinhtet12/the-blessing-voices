Parables & Metaphors

The Door of the Sheep

Consider Jesus’ image of the door through which the sheep find safety and pasture.

John 10:7–9

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Jesus described himself as the door for the sheep. He contrasted his role with thieves and robbers whom the sheep did not listen to. He said that whoever enters through him will be saved and will go in and out and find pasture. This is a paraphrase of John 10:7–9.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The image appears within the sheep-and-shepherd teaching of John 10. Some translations use gate rather than door. The passage does not require an invented story of a particular pen or a named sheep.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The image invites reflection on trusting Jesus and receiving the life and care he offers.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Read the surrounding teaching slowly. Reflect on the voices you trust for guidance and consider whether they lead you toward truth, love, and faithful action.

Reflection Questions

  1. What does Jesus say about entering through him?
  2. What does the image of pasture suggest within this teaching?
  3. How can you attend more carefully to Jesus’ guidance?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us understand your Word with humility. Teach us to grow in faith, care for one another, and put what we learn into practice. Amen.
