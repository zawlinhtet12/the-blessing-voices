Jesus Heals a Lame Man at the Pool of Bethesda

Read how Jesus noticed a man who had been ill for thirty-eight years at Bethesda.

John 5:1–15

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

In Jerusalem, Jesus saw a man at the pool of Bethesda who had been ill for thirty-eight years. He asked whether the man wanted to get well. The man explained that he had no one to help him into the pool when the water was stirred. Jesus told him to get up, take his mat, and walk. At once he was healed and did so. It was the Sabbath, and a dispute arose about carrying his mat. Later Jesus found him in the temple and warned him against continuing in sin. This is a paraphrase of John 5:1–15.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The account locates the pool near the Sheep Gate. Its focus is Jesus’ encounter with the man and the response to the Sabbath healing. The man’s explanation about the water is reported as his statement rather than proof of a general healing method.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The story draws attention to Jesus noticing a person with a long-standing need, his effective command, and a call to changed living.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Notice people facing long-term barriers. Ask what practical support is missing and offer appropriate help without making assumptions about the cause of illness or disability.

Reflection Questions

  1. What does Jesus ask the man?
  2. What obstacle does the man describe?
  3. What barrier could you help someone overcome in daily life?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for your mercy. Help us listen to your teaching and respond to others with gratitude, patience, and compassion. Amen.
