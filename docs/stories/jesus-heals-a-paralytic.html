Jesus Heals a Paralytic

Read how Jesus forgave and healed a paralysed man brought to him by others.

Matthew 9:1–8; Mark 2:1–12; Luke 5:17–26

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

In Capernaum, people gathered to hear Jesus in a crowded house. Four men brought a paralysed man but could not reach Jesus through the crowd. They opened the roof and lowered the man on his mat. Seeing their faith, Jesus told the man that his sins were forgiven. Some teachers of the law questioned this in their hearts. Jesus addressed their thoughts and declared that the Son of Man had authority on earth to forgive sins. He told the man to get up, take his mat, and go home. The man rose, picked up his mat, and walked out before everyone, who were amazed and praised God. This paraphrase follows Mark 2:1–12.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Matthew 9:1–8 and Luke 5:17–26 record parallel accounts. The roof opening and four bearers come from Mark. The words about forgiveness appear in Mark 2:5, Matthew 9:2, and Luke 5:20. The narrative does not state that the man’s paralysis was caused by a particular personal sin.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The account highlights Jesus’ authority to forgive, the visible healing, and the faith expressed by those bringing the man. Their actions also invite devotional reflection on persistent care for another person.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Consider how you can help someone overcome a practical barrier to participation in community life. Bring your own need for forgiveness honestly to God. Offer support without making assumptions about why another person is ill or disabled.

Reflection Questions

  1. What do the four bearers do when they cannot reach Jesus?
  2. How are forgiveness and Jesus’ authority central to the account?
  3. What practical barrier could you help someone overcome this week?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for your mercy and forgiveness. Help us support one another with persistent care and honour every person’s dignity. Amen.
