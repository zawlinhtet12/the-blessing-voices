Parables & Metaphors

Fruit of the Spirit (Paul)

Reflect on the character of a life shaped by the Spirit.

Galatians 5:22–23

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Paul names the fruit of the Spirit as love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control, and says there is no law against such things. This is a paraphrase of Galatians 5:22–23.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The fruit image appears within Paul’s discussion of life in the Spirit in Galatians 5. It concerns character, not literal varieties of fruit or a ranking of people by spiritual worth.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Spiritual growth should be expressed in the way people live and relate to others. The listed qualities belong together as the fruit of life shaped by the Spirit.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Choose one quality to attend to in a difficult relationship. Ask for God’s help and identify a small action that gives it practical expression.

Reflection Questions

  1. Which qualities does Paul name?
  2. How might these qualities shape a community’s relationships?
  3. Which quality needs particular attention in your life this week?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us receive your Word with humility and put it into practice. Shape our words, choices, and relationships with truth, faithfulness, and love. Amen.
