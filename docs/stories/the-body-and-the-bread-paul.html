Parables & Metaphors

The Body and the Bread (Paul)

Reflect on shared bread, participation in Christ, and remembrance at the Lord’s Supper.

1 Corinthians 10:16–17; 11:23–26

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Paul speaks of the cup of blessing as participation in Christ’s blood and the bread that is broken as participation in his body. He connects the one bread with the many being one body. In a later passage, he recounts Jesus taking bread, giving thanks, breaking it, and calling for remembrance, along with the cup as the new covenant in his blood. Paul says that sharing the bread and cup proclaims the Lord’s death until he comes. This is a paraphrase of 1 Corinthians 10:16–17 and 11:23–26.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

These passages occur in Paul’s teaching to the Corinthian church. The surrounding discussion includes shared participation and conduct at the Lord’s Supper. This page presents the passage without inventing a modern ceremony or settling differences among Christian traditions.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The imagery invites remembrance, shared belonging, and reverence for Christ’s self-giving.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Reflect on how you treat those with whom you worship. Seek reconciliation and practise care that expresses shared belonging.

Reflection Questions

  1. How does Paul connect the one bread with the one body?
  2. What does sharing the bread and cup proclaim?
  3. How can your conduct express care for fellow worshippers?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us receive your Word with humility and put it into practice. Shape our words, choices, and relationships with truth, faithfulness, and love. Amen.
