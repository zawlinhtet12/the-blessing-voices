Jesus Heals Bartimaeus, a Blind Beggar

Read how Bartimaeus called for mercy and followed Jesus after receiving his sight.

Mark 10:46–52; related account: Luke 18:35–43

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

As Jesus was leaving Jericho with his disciples and a large crowd, Bartimaeus, a blind beggar, sat beside the road. Hearing that Jesus was passing, he called to the Son of David for mercy. Many rebuked him, but he cried out even more. Jesus stopped and called for him. Bartimaeus threw off his cloak, rose, and came. Jesus asked what he wanted; he asked to see again. Jesus affirmed his faith. Immediately he received his sight and followed Jesus on the road. This paraphrase follows Mark 10:46–52.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Mark names Bartimaeus and sets the encounter as Jesus leaves Jericho. Luke 18:35–43 describes a related healing as Jesus approaches Jericho. Read each account on its own terms rather than inventing details to reconcile the settings.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The story highlights an appeal for mercy, Jesus’ willingness to stop, and the man’s response of following him. Jesus listens to Bartimaeus’ own request.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Listen to a person whose needs are being dismissed. Ask what help they want rather than assuming you know. Consider how gratitude can lead to faithful action.

Reflection Questions

  1. How does Bartimaeus respond when others try to silence him?
  2. Why is Jesus’ question to him important?
  3. How can you help someone be heard rather than overlooked?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for your mercy. Help us listen to your teaching and respond to others with gratitude, patience, and compassion. Amen.
