The Parable of the Lost Coin

Reflect on the careful search for a lost coin and the joy when it is found.

Luke 15:8–10

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical passage alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Jesus described a woman with ten silver coins who lost one. She lit a lamp, swept the house, and searched carefully until she found it. Then she called her friends and neighbours to rejoice with her. Jesus spoke of joy in the presence of God’s angels over one sinner who repents. This is a paraphrase of Luke 15:8–10.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The Lost Coin follows the Lost Sheep in Luke 15. The passage does not describe the woman’s marital status or identify the coins as a particular ceremonial object.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Careful seeking and shared rejoicing illuminate the value of the lost and the joy of repentance.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Give patient attention to someone who is easily overlooked. Celebrate another person’s change of direction with encouragement.

Reflection Questions

  1. How does the woman search for the coin?
  2. What does Jesus say this joy illustrates?
  3. How can you encourage someone taking a new step toward God?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us hear your Word with humility. Guide our choices and teach us to respond with faith, mercy, and faithful action. Amen.
