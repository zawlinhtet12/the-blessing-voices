Jesus Heals a Man with Dropsy

Reflect on Jesus’ compassion and his question about healing on the Sabbath.

Luke 14:1–6

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

On a Sabbath, Jesus went to eat at the house of a leader of the Pharisees, where he was being closely watched. A man with dropsy was before him. Jesus asked the lawyers and Pharisees whether healing on the Sabbath was lawful. They remained silent. He took hold of the man, healed him, and sent him away. Jesus then asked about immediately rescuing a son or an ox that had fallen into a well on the Sabbath. They could not answer. This is a paraphrase of Luke 14:1–6.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Luke places the healing within a meal and a discussion about Sabbath practice. The passage does not name the man or explain the cause of his condition.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The account invites reflection on mercy, doing good, and the consistency between what we permit for ourselves and the care we offer others.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Consider a need you may be overlooking. Offer thoughtful practical help, and examine whether convenience or fear of criticism is restricting your compassion.

Reflection Questions

  1. What question does Jesus ask before the healing?
  2. How does his rescue example challenge the listeners?
  3. What practical act of compassion could you offer this week?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for your mercy. Help us listen to your teaching and respond to others with gratitude, patience, and compassion. Amen.
