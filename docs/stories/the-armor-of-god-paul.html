Parables & Metaphors

The Armor of God (Paul)

Consider the armour imagery of truth, righteousness, faith, salvation, God’s word, and prayer.

Ephesians 6:10–18

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Ephesians calls believers to be strong in the Lord and put on God’s armour so that they can stand against the devil’s schemes. It describes truth as a belt, righteousness as a breastplate, readiness from the gospel of peace for the feet, faith as a shield, salvation as a helmet, and the word of God as the Spirit’s sword. The passage also calls for prayer, alertness, and perseverance for all the saints. This is a paraphrase of Ephesians 6:10–18.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The passage explicitly says the struggle is not against flesh and blood. The armour is a spiritual illustration; it does not authorize hostility or physical violence against other people.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The teaching connects steadfastness with truth, righteousness, peace, trust, salvation, God’s word, and prayer rather than self-reliance.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Choose one practice of truthfulness, peace-making, or prayer to strengthen this week. Pray for others as well as for yourself.

Reflection Questions

  1. What does the passage say about the nature of the struggle?
  2. Which part of the armour helps you reflect on a present need?
  3. How can you practise peace and perseverance this week?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us understand your Word with humility. Teach us to grow in faith, care for one another, and put what we learn into practice. Amen.
