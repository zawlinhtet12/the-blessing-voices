Jesus Heals Ten Lepers

Discover the gratitude of the one man who returned to thank Jesus after ten were cleansed.

Luke 17:11–19

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

On his way to Jerusalem, Jesus encountered ten men with leprosy who stood at a distance and called for mercy. He told them to show themselves to the priests. As they went, they were cleansed. One, seeing he was healed, returned praising God loudly and fell at Jesus’ feet to thank him. He was a Samaritan. Jesus asked about the other nine and addressed the returning man’s faith, sending him on his way. This is a paraphrase of Luke 17:11–19.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Luke specifically identifies the returning man as a Samaritan. The account records the cleansing of all ten and gives special attention to the one who returned; it does not describe what each of the other nine later did.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Mercy, trust, gratitude, and praise are central themes. The thankful response comes from someone Jesus describes as a foreigner.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Make time to express gratitude to God and to people who have helped you. Notice kindness beyond familiar social boundaries.

Reflection Questions

  1. What happens as the ten men follow Jesus’ instruction?
  2. What makes the returning man’s response stand out?
  3. How could you express gratitude more intentionally today?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for your mercy. Help us listen to your teaching and respond to others with gratitude, patience, and compassion. Amen.
