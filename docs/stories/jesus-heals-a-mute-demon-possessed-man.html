Jesus Heals a Mute Demon-Possessed Man

Explore the healing of a man unable to speak and the contrasting responses to Jesus’ work.

Matthew 9:32–34

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

A man who was unable to speak and was demon-possessed was brought to Jesus. After the demon was driven out, the man spoke. The crowd marvelled, saying that nothing like this had been seen in Israel. The Pharisees accused Jesus of driving out demons by the ruler of demons. This is a paraphrase of Matthew 9:32–34.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Matthew records this brief account immediately after the healing of two blind men. It describes a particular case of demon possession and does not identify all speech disabilities with demonic activity.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The passage portrays Jesus’ authority and shows contrasting responses to the same event: amazement from the crowd and accusation from the Pharisees.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Listen carefully before making judgments. Include people who communicate differently and take time to understand their needs. Respond to vulnerability with respect and compassion.

Reflection Questions

  1. What changes for the man in this account?
  2. How do the crowd and the Pharisees respond differently?
  3. How can you help someone’s voice be heard in your community?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for your compassion. Help us trust you, listen carefully to your teaching, and care for others with patience, kindness, and respect. Amen.
