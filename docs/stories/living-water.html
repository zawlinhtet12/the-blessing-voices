Parables & Metaphors

Living Water

Explore Jesus’ promise of living water and John’s explanation of the Spirit.

John 4:10–14; John 7:37–39

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

In his conversation with the Samaritan woman, Jesus spoke of the gift of God and living water. He contrasted ordinary water, after which a person thirsts again, with the water he gives, which becomes a spring leading to eternal life. This is a paraphrase of John 4:10–14.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

John 7:37–39 contains a separate invitation to the thirsty to come to Jesus and drink. John explains that this refers to the Spirit whom believers would receive. The two passages share imagery but belong to different occasions.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The water imagery invites reflection on God’s gift, life in Christ, and the Spirit. It expresses a spiritual promise rather than a claim that ordinary bodily needs disappear.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Bring your deepest longings to God in prayer. Read both passages in their settings and consider how receiving God’s gift shapes your care for others.

Reflection Questions

  1. How does Jesus contrast ordinary water with the water he gives?
  2. How does John explain the image in John 7?
  3. What longing or need would you like to bring to God honestly?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us understand your Word and receive it with humility. Guide our choices and teach us to live with faith, wisdom, and love. Amen.
