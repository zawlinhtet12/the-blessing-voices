Parables & Metaphors

Light of the World

Consider how visible good works can direct praise to the Father.

Matthew 5:14–16

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Jesus called his disciples the light of the world. He spoke of a city on a hill that cannot be hidden and a lamp placed on a stand to give light. He urged them to let their light shine so that others would see their good works and glorify their Father in heaven. This is a paraphrase of Matthew 5:14–16.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

This study concerns Jesus’ words to his disciples in Matthew. John 8:12 also uses light language when Jesus describes himself, but that is a distinct saying in a different setting.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The teaching connects visible conduct with praise directed to God. Its focus does not require seeking admiration for oneself.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Do a concrete act of service with humility. Consider how your words and actions affect the way others experience Christian faith.

Reflection Questions

  1. What result does Jesus desire when people see good works?
  2. How can visible service remain humble?
  3. What act of kindness could you put into practice today?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us understand your Word and receive it with humility. Guide our choices and teach us to live with faith, wisdom, and love. Amen.
