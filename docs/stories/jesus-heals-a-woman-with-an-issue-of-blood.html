Jesus Heals a Woman with an Issue of Blood

Reflect on Jesus’ compassionate response to a woman who had suffered bleeding for twelve years.

Matthew 9:20–22; Mark 5:25–34; Luke 8:43–48

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

A woman who had experienced bleeding for twelve years came through the crowd and touched Jesus’ clothing, believing she would be healed. Immediately her bleeding stopped. Jesus became aware that power had gone out from him and asked who had touched his clothes. The woman came forward trembling and told him the truth. He addressed her as daughter, affirmed her faith, and sent her away in peace. This paraphrase follows Mark 5:25–34.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Matthew and Luke also record the healing. Mark describes her long suffering and her approach through the crowd. The narrative centres on this particular woman’s encounter with Jesus; it does not say that everyone who remains ill lacks faith.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The account invites reflection on trust, compassion, and the dignity of being personally acknowledged. Jesus listens to the woman rather than allowing her story to remain anonymous.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Listen patiently to someone living with long-term suffering. Bring your needs honestly to God and offer care without judging another person’s faith.

Reflection Questions

  1. What stands out about the woman’s approach to Jesus?
  2. How does Jesus address her after she comes forward?
  3. How can you offer patient care to someone who has suffered for a long time?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for your compassion. Help us trust you, listen carefully to your teaching, and care for others with patience, kindness, and respect. Amen.
