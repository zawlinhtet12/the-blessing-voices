Jesus Heals the Ear of Malchus

Reflect on Jesus’ healing response during the violence surrounding his arrest.

Luke 22:47–53; John 18:10–11

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

During Jesus’ arrest, one of those with him struck the high priest’s servant and cut off his right ear. Jesus stopped the violence, touched the servant’s ear, and healed him. This healing is recorded in Luke 22:47–53. John 18:10–11 identifies the disciple as Simon Peter and the servant as Malchus, and records Jesus telling Peter to put away his sword.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Luke records the healing, while John supplies the names Peter and Malchus. These details are attributed to their respective accounts. The passages do not tell us Malchus’ later response or life story.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Jesus responds with care amid hostility and restrains a follower’s violence. The scene invites reflection on mercy and refusing retaliation.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Pause before responding in anger. Seek a peaceful way to address conflict and offer care without making compassion depend on someone being on your side.

Reflection Questions

  1. How does Jesus respond to the injury?
  2. What do Luke and John each contribute to the account?
  3. How can you practise restraint and mercy in a conflict?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for your mercy. Help us listen to your teaching and respond to others with gratitude, patience, and compassion. Amen.
