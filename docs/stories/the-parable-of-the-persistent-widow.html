The Parable of the Persistent Widow

Find encouragement to pray without losing heart through the widow who persisted in seeking justice.

Luke 18:1–8

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical passage alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Jesus told a story about a judge who neither feared God nor respected people. A widow kept coming to him asking for justice against her adversary. At first he refused, but eventually he decided to grant her justice because she kept troubling him. Jesus contrasted this judge with God’s response to his chosen ones who cry to him, and asked whether the Son of Man would find faith on earth when he came. This is a paraphrase of Luke 18:1–8.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Luke states the purpose of the parable: to pray always and not lose heart. The judge is unjust; the comparison does not describe God as unwilling or corrupt.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The passage encourages persistent prayer, hope for justice, and enduring faith.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Keep bringing a concern to God with honesty. Support someone seeking fair treatment and resist discouragement without treating persistence as control over God.

Reflection Questions

  1. Why does the judge eventually act?
  2. How does Jesus contrast the judge with God?
  3. What concern can you continue bringing to God in prayer?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us hear your Word with humility. Guide our choices and teach us to respond with faith, mercy, and faithful action. Amen.
