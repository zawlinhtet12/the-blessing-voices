Parables & Metaphors

The Way, the Truth, and the Life

Reflect on Jesus’ answer to Thomas and his teaching about coming to the Father.

John 14:6

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

When Thomas asked how the disciples could know the way, Jesus described himself as the way, the truth, and the life, and said that no one comes to the Father except through him. This paraphrase follows John 14:5–6.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The statement belongs to Jesus’ words to his disciples in John 14. Read it alongside their questions and his call to trust. It presents his identity rather than a fictional journey with named roads.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The saying directs attention to relationship with Jesus and the Father. Its three images invite reflection on guidance, truth, and life.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Bring questions to God honestly. Consider one area where following Jesus’ teaching could shape a concrete decision.

Reflection Questions

  1. What question does Thomas ask?
  2. How does Jesus answer with reference to himself?
  3. What decision in your life calls for faithful guidance?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us understand your Word with humility. Teach us to grow in faith, care for one another, and put what we learn into practice. Amen.
