Jesus as the Cornerstone (Peter)

Consider the cornerstone image and the call to trust Christ.

1 Peter 2:6–8

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

First Peter draws on Scripture to describe a chosen, precious cornerstone in Zion, with the promise that the one who trusts in him will not be put to shame. The passage contrasts honour for believers with the rejected stone becoming the cornerstone, and with a stone over which people stumble. This is a paraphrase of 1 Peter 2:6–8.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The cornerstone language develops the living-stone image in the preceding verses and draws together scriptural stone imagery. It is a theological illustration, not an account of a named construction project.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The passage invites reflection on Christ’s central place, trust, and responses to him. Its warning calls for humble attention rather than contempt toward other people.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Consider what anchors your choices. Read the surrounding passage and choose one way to express trust in Christ through faithful conduct.

Reflection Questions

  1. How is the cornerstone described?
  2. What contrasting responses does the passage present?
  3. What could it mean for Christ to shape the foundation of your decisions?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us receive your Word with humility and put it into practice. Shape our words, choices, and relationships with truth, faithfulness, and love. Amen.
