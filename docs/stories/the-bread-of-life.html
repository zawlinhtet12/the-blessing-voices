Parables & Metaphors

The Bread of Life

Reflect on Jesus’ declaration that he is the bread of life and his invitation to come and believe.

John 6:35

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Jesus declared that he is the bread of life. He spoke of those who come to him not hungering and those who believe in him not thirsting. This is a paraphrase of John 6:35.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

This saying belongs to the extended bread-of-life teaching in John 6. Its language points to receiving life through Jesus, rather than a claim that believers no longer need ordinary food or drink.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The image invites reflection on dependence on Christ, coming to him, and believing in him.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Bring your deepest needs to God in prayer. Consider what you rely on for lasting security and make time to listen to Jesus’ teaching.

Reflection Questions

  1. What does Jesus call himself in this saying?
  2. How are coming and believing connected with the image?
  3. What need would you like to bring to God honestly?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us understand your Word with humility. Teach us to grow in faith, care for one another, and put what we learn into practice. Amen.
