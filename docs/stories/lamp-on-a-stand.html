Parables & Metaphors

Lamp on a Stand

Reflect on a lamp placed where its light can be useful and on listening carefully to Jesus.

Matthew 5:15–16; Mark 4:21–25; Luke 8:16–18

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Jesus described people lighting a lamp and placing it on a stand rather than hiding it under a basket. In Matthew, the lamp gives light to everyone in the house, followed by the call to let good works lead others to glorify the Father. This paraphrase follows Matthew 5:15–16.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Mark 4:21–25 and Luke 8:16–18 use related lamp imagery in teachings about disclosure and attentive hearing. The passages should be read in their own settings rather than merged into a single invented conversation.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The image invites reflection on useful, visible faith and, in the related readings, careful listening. A gift or opportunity can serve others when it is put to good use.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Use a skill or resource to help someone. Read the related passages slowly and notice what each setting emphasizes.

Reflection Questions

  1. Where is the lamp placed, and why?
  2. How do the related passages connect the image with hearing?
  3. What ability could you use to serve another person?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us understand your Word and receive it with humility. Guide our choices and teach us to live with faith, wisdom, and love. Amen.
