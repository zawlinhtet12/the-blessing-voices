The Parable of the Cost of Discipleship

Consider Jesus’ call to count the cost of following him through the illustrations of a builder and a king.

Luke 14:25–33

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical passage alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Speaking to large crowds, Jesus described the demanding commitment of discipleship, including carrying one’s cross and following him. He asked whether a person planning a tower would first estimate the cost to make sure it could be finished. He also described a king considering whether his army could face a larger force, and seeking peace if it could not. Jesus concluded by speaking of relinquishing possessions to be his disciple. This is a paraphrase of Luke 14:25–33.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The tower and king are two illustrations within a teaching about discipleship, rather than a single named person’s life story. Read the demanding sayings within the whole passage.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The passage calls for deliberate commitment rather than an impulsive promise. Following Jesus concerns the priorities and attachments that shape a person’s life.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Reflect honestly on your commitments. Choose one faithful step you can sustain and consider what competing attachment needs to change.

Reflection Questions

  1. Why does the builder estimate the cost?
  2. What do the two illustrations contribute to Jesus’ teaching?
  3. What priority in your life needs careful reconsideration?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us hear your Word with humility. Guide our choices and teach us to respond with faith, mercy, and faithful action. Amen.
