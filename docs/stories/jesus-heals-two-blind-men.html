Miracles

Jesus Heals Two Blind Men

Read how two blind men called to Jesus for mercy and received their sight.

Matthew 9:27–31

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Two blind men followed Jesus, calling him Son of David and asking for mercy. When he entered a house, they came to him. Jesus asked whether they believed he could do this, and they affirmed that they did. He touched their eyes and spoke about their faith; their sight was restored. He warned them to keep it quiet, but they spread the news throughout the region. This is a paraphrase of Matthew 9:27–31.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

This account in Matthew 9 is distinct from the healing of two blind men near Jericho in Matthew 20:29–34. The passage records both their appeal for mercy and Jesus’ instruction after the healing.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The story highlights an appeal for mercy and trust in Jesus. It also invites attention to listening carefully to what Jesus asks, rather than focusing only on receiving help.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Pray with humility and listen attentively to Scripture. Respect the dignity and independence of people with visual disabilities, and ask what assistance would be welcome.

Reflection Questions

  1. Why do the men call out to Jesus?
  2. What question does Jesus ask them?
  3. What would respectful, practical support for another person look like this week?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for your compassion. Help us trust you, listen carefully to your teaching, and care for others with patience, kindness, and respect. Amen.
